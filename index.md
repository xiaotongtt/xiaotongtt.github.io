<h2 id="about">About Me</h2>

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My research lies at the intersection of **embodied AI**, **generative AI and multimodal learning**, and **efficient vision models**. My current research interests focus on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) and world models.

I received my Ph.D. and M.Eng. degrees in Computer Science and Technology from Xiamen University. I received my B.Eng. degree in Electronic Science and Technology from Xiamen University.

My earlier research focused on image and video restoration, diffusion models, model compression, and adaptive inference. I have published 20+ papers in venues including TPAMI, CVPR, ECCV, NeurIPS, AAAI, and ACM MM. I am now building on this foundation to study data quality, cross-embodiment transfer, and efficient learning for real-world robotic systems.
